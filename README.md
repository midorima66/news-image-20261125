One image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026, drawn
as a newspaper front page: the cyber breach at the CIA and the Office of
the Director of National Intelligence, a Houthi drone strike wounding 11
people at Riyadh's tallest tower, a US jury finding Huawei guilty of
stealing trade secrets, the Solomon Islands signing a new security treaty
with Australia as it drops its pact with China, Ukraine confirming its
drone strike on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II, and the WHO evacuating
its staff from an Ebola hospital in the Democratic Republic of the Congo
as fighting closed in.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program copies `entry.png` (1024 by 1024, under 1 MiB) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, or to `/output/entry.png` when the
variable is unset. It reads nothing else and writes the same bytes on
every run.
